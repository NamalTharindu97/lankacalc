# Sri Lanka Web Application Service Opportunities
## Market Research, Feasibility Study, and Product Strategy

**Market:** Sri Lanka  
**Platform focus:** Web applications / SaaS / browser-based services  
**Research date:** August 14, 2026  
**Purpose:** Identify useful online services that can be built for Sri Lankan users, with emphasis on underserved problems, monetization potential, and realistic execution for a software developer.

---

## Executive Summary

Sri Lanka already has many mobile apps, marketplaces, government portals, financial calculators, classified websites, and individual online services. Therefore, the strongest opportunities are not simply to copy an existing international product.

The better opportunity is to build **Sri Lanka-specific web applications that combine fragmented information, simplify difficult processes, or help users make decisions**.

The most promising categories identified in this research are:

1. **Sri Lanka Calculator / Decision Platform**
2. **Supermarket Basket Price Comparison**
3. **Government Service & Document Assistant**
4. **SME Compliance Assistant**
5. **Employee Finance / Job Offer Decision Platform**
6. **Construction Material Price Intelligence**
7. **Vehicle Ownership Cost Platform**
8. **Freelancer Finance Assistant**
9. **Medicine Price & Availability Search**
10. **Sri Lanka Price History Platform**
11. **Cost-of-Living & Rent Decision Platform**
12. **Government / Vehicle Renewal Reminder Service**
13. **Complaint Routing Assistant**
14. **Education / Course Comparison Platform**
15. **Agricultural Price Intelligence Platform**

The strongest recommendation is to begin with a service that:

- works fully in the browser;
- does not require a two-sided marketplace at launch;
- can be discovered through Google;
- solves a recurring Sri Lankan problem;
- can be expanded gradually; and
- has a clear monetization path.

For a solo developer, the best first projects are:

> **LankaCalc / WorkMoney**, **GovGuide**, **PriceLK**, **SME Compliance**, and **BuildPrice**.

# 1. Research Objective

The purpose of this study is to identify web applications that can provide meaningful value to Sri Lankan users.

The research focuses on applications that are:

- browser-based;
- useful without requiring mobile installation;
- Sri Lanka-specific;
- scalable;
- SEO-friendly where appropriate;
- monetizable;
- technically realistic; and
- capable of being launched incrementally.

This study also distinguishes between:

- services that already exist strongly;
- services that exist but are fragmented;
- services where the complete solution appears underserved.

---

# 2. Important Market Principle

A service does **not** need to be completely new to succeed.

A better question is:

> “Is the current Sri Lankan solution good enough?”

Many valuable opportunities exist because users currently need to:

- visit several government websites;
- search multiple shops separately;
- read PDFs;
- manually calculate costs;
- compare information in spreadsheets;
- call businesses;
- search Facebook groups;
- use several unrelated apps;
- remember deadlines manually.

A strong web application can consolidate these tasks.

# 4. LankaCalc – Sri Lanka Calculation and Decision Platform

## Problem

Sri Lankan users regularly need locally specific calculations involving:

- salary;
- APIT;
- EPF;
- ETF;
- gratuity;
- overtime;
- loans;
- leasing;
- electricity;
- fuel;
- vehicle imports;
- construction;
- solar;
- tax;
- business expenses.

Many calculators exist independently, but users still need a reliable platform that explains how results were calculated.

## Product Concept

```text
LankaCalc.lk

Salary
APIT
EPF / ETF
Gratuity
OT
Loan
Lease
Electricity
Vehicle Import
Fuel
Solar
Construction
Business
```

## Key Differentiator

Do not compete only on calculator quantity.

Compete on:

- Sri Lanka-specific calculations;
- verified formulas;
- official sources;
- rule versions;
- last-verified dates;
- calculation breakdowns;
- Sinhala / English / Tamil;
- useful decision support.

## Important Architecture

Some formulas are permanent mathematical formulas.

Others can change.

### Static

- age;
- percentage;
- loan EMI formula;
- compound interest;
- area;
- tile quantity;
- fuel consumption.

### Configurable

- fuel price;
- bank interest rates;
- construction material assumptions;
- solar costs.

### Regulated

- APIT;
- EPF;
- ETF;
- electricity tariffs;
- gratuity;
- Customs;
- vehicle duties;
- employment-related calculations.

Regulated formulas should use a versioned rule system.

## Revenue

- AdSense;
- loan leads;
- leasing leads;
- solar leads;
- construction leads;
- sponsored calculators;
- PDF reports;
- calculator API;
- embeddable B2B widgets.

# 5. WorkMoney.lk – Employee Financial Decision Platform

This could either be part of LankaCalc or a separate focused brand.

## Services

```text
Salary Calculator
Take-Home Calculator
Net-to-Gross Calculator
APIT
EPF
ETF
Gratuity
OT
Salary Increment
Job Offer Comparison
Loan Affordability
```

## Killer Feature: Job Offer Comparison

A user enters current-job and new-job values such as gross salary, bonuses, travel expenses, WFH days, and allowances.

The system calculates:

- estimated annual take-home;
- additional tax;
- travel difference;
- yearly bonus difference;
- employer contributions;
- real annual financial improvement.

Instead of saying:

> “Your salary increased by Rs. 70,000.”

it can say:

> “Your estimated real annual financial improvement is Rs. XXX,XXX.”

## Revenue

- job-platform partnerships;
- recruitment leads;
- financial products;
- premium reports;
- salary insights;
- employer tools.

# 6. PriceLK – Supermarket Basket Price Comparison

## Problem

A Sri Lankan consumer may have to individually check Cargills, Keells, Glomark, Arpico, SPAR, Sathosa, and other retailers.

There is value in combining these prices.

## Basic Search

```text
Anchor Full Cream Milk Powder 1kg
```

Example result:

```text
Retailer A     Rs. X
Retailer B     Rs. X
Retailer C     Rs. X

Cheapest: Retailer B
```

## Stronger Feature: Basket Optimizer

User enters a complete shopping basket.

The system calculates:

- each retailer's full basket price;
- cheapest complete basket;
- potential saving;
- cheapest split purchase across stores.

## Challenges

The main difficulty is not coding. It is:

- obtaining retailer data;
- matching identical products;
- handling sizes;
- handling offers;
- detecting unavailable items;
- preventing stale prices.

## Revenue

- retailer partnerships;
- FMCG advertising;
- sponsored offers;
- affiliate/referral traffic;
- premium price alerts;
- analytics for brands.

# 8. GovGuide.lk – Government Service Assistant

## Problem

Government services are distributed across different agencies and portals.

Users often ask:

- What documents do I need?
- Which office should I visit?
- What form should I use?
- How much does it cost?
- Can I do it online?
- What happens if my document is lost?

## Product Idea

User enters a situation such as:

```text
I lost my NIC
```

The web application asks relevant questions and produces:

- personalized checklist;
- documents required;
- fees;
- steps;
- office / online service;
- forms;
- official links;
- last verified date.

## Services to Cover

- NIC;
- passport;
- driving licence;
- revenue licence;
- birth certificate;
- marriage certificate;
- death certificate;
- police clearance;
- TIN;
- business registration;
- EPF;
- ETF;
- vehicle ownership procedures.

## Important Strategy

Do not pretend to be a government website.

The service should clearly act as:

> **a guidance layer that sends users to the correct official service.**

# 15. MedPrice.lk – Medicine Price and Availability Search

## Product

Search medicine name, dosage, and strength.

Then show:

- official price information where available;
- pharmacy prices;
- pharmacy availability;
- nearby stock.

## Challenges

This service is operationally harder because it requires:

- accurate stock data;
- pharmacy partnerships;
- product identity matching;
- careful handling of prescription products;
- strong medical disclaimers;
- no unsafe medical recommendations.

Because of health-related risk, this is not recommended as a first solo project.

# 19. Services That Are Less Attractive as First Projects

## Lost & Found

Sri Lankan lost-and-found platforms already exist. A new service would need a significantly better distribution strategy or integration advantage.

## Generic Home-Service Marketplace

The market already contains multiple service-booking platforms.

The biggest problem is not development. It is:

- onboarding workers;
- quality assurance;
- trust;
- customer acquisition;
- dispute management;
- marketplace liquidity.

## Generic Construction Calculator

Existing construction estimators are already available.

A stronger product would combine:

> **prices + supplier comparison + estimation + quotations**

rather than just a calculator.

## Generic Salary Calculator

Many versions already exist.

A stronger product would provide:

> **employee decision tools**, not just salary arithmetic.

# 20. Web-First Product Strategy

A major advantage of these ideas is that they can work without installing an app.

Ideal user flow:

```text
Google Search
     ↓
Specific web page
     ↓
Use service immediately
     ↓
Receive result
```

Potential searches include:

```text
salary calculator sri lanka
passport renewal documents sri lanka
cheapest supermarket sri lanka
cement price sri lanka
vehicle ownership cost sri lanka
job offer comparison sri lanka
```

This creates organic search opportunities.

# 25. Recommended Build Order for a Solo Developer

## Option A – Lowest Operational Risk

1. LankaCalc / WorkMoney
2. GovGuide
3. LankaDeadline

These rely mainly on software, research, SEO, content, and rule management.

They do not require large supplier networks.

## Option B – Highest Consumer Opportunity

Start with PriceLK and later expand into PriceHistory, offers, price alerts, and shopping basket optimization.

The difficulty is reliable data acquisition.

## Option C – Strongest SaaS Revenue

Start with the SME Compliance Assistant.

This has fewer users than a public calculator website, but companies may pay monthly.

## Option D – Strongest Lead-Generation Opportunity

Start with BuildPrice or VehicleCost.

These users often have high commercial intent.

---

# 26. Recommended Overall Strategy

Do not attempt to build 15 products at once.

Choose one vertical and prove:

1. Users need it.
2. Users can find it.
3. Users return.
4. Data can be maintained.
5. Monetization is possible.

Then expand.

# 28. Final Recommendation

The strongest opportunities are services that take fragmented Sri Lankan information and turn it into a simple answer.

Examples:

Instead of:

> “Here are several government pages.”

Provide:

> “Based on your situation, here are the exact documents you need.”

Instead of:

> “Here are supermarket websites.”

Provide:

> “Your full basket is cheapest at this retailer today.”

Instead of:

> “Here is a salary calculator.”

Provide:

> “This new job would increase your real annual income by approximately Rs. X.”

Instead of:

> “Here are construction products.”

Provide:

> “Your estimated project needs these materials, these are current price ranges, and these suppliers can quote.”

That is where the strongest Sri Lankan web-service opportunities appear to exist.

# Conclusion

Sri Lanka does not necessarily need more generic applications.

The strongest web opportunities are services that are:

> **local, accurate, searchable, understandable, and decision-oriented.**

A solo developer can realistically build a valuable Sri Lankan web service if the project begins with a narrow problem, launches quickly, collects real usage data, and expands only after the first service proves demand.

The recommended starting point remains:

> **LankaCalc / WorkMoney → validate traffic and trust → expand into additional Sri Lankan utility services.**
